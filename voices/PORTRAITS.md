# Portraits for Voices of the Ladder

Ten portraits, one style, generated with the same image pipeline as the Jacob’s Dream gallery. The page crops each one into its arch, so every prompt asks for a plain vertical portrait with room above the head and no frame of its own.

**Style: the gallery’s Illuminated Manuscript.** It suits everyone on the grid. Ramban and Rambam were both Sephardic, Rashi is medieval, and the style reads as clearly imagined, which matters because no one knows what Rashi, Ramban, or Rambam looked like. Jewish Papercut is the alternative if you want something folksier for the younger grades; I can rewrite the prompts for it.

**Where they go:** save each as a JPG, 1200 × 1600 pixels, named by id (`jacob.jpg`, `esau.jpg`, …) in `voices/portraits/`. I’ll connect them.

## The shared part of every prompt

Put this first, then the character’s own lines:

> Vertical 3:4 portrait. A miniature painting in the style of a 14th-century Sephardic Hebrew Bible from medieval Spain: flat jewel-toned pigments (lapis blue, vermilion, malachite green) with burnished gold leaf, fine black ink outlines, aged vellum texture. One figure, seen from the chest up, centered, facing slightly to the side, with generous empty space above the head. Plain background: a flat field of the color named below with a few small gold stars. No frame, no border, no arch. No halo and no gold disc behind anyone’s head. No crosses, no church architecture, no doves, no radiating rays. No words, letters, numbers, or writing of any kind; no signature.

## The ten characters

**Jacob** (`jacob.jpg`). Background: deep indigo night.
> A young traveler in a patterned wool cloak and a plain head cloth, a short dark beard, a walking staff resting against his shoulder. He looks up and slightly away, tired but full of wonder, as if he has just woken from a dream. In one hand, a smooth gray stone.

**Esau** (`esau.jpg`). Background: warm red-brown, like earth at dusk.
> Jacob’s twin: a strong young outdoorsman with ruddy skin, thick reddish hair and beard, and hairy forearms. A hunting bow over one shoulder and a leather quiver. His expression is hurt and proud, not cruel: a man who has just lost something that mattered to him.

**Rebekah** (`rebekah.jpg`). Background: deep teal, like well water.
> A dignified woman in middle age, in a draped headscarf and patterned robe, holding a clay water jar against her hip. She looks into the distance with worried love, as if watching someone walk away.

**Isaac** (`isaac.jpg`). Background: warm plum-brown lamplight.
> An old man with a long white beard, seated, his eyes softly closed because he can no longer see. His hands are open and raised a little in front of him, in the gesture of giving a blessing. Gentle, tired, kind.

**An angel** (`angel.jpg`). Background: deep blue night with more gold stars.
> Not a person at all: a single small golden flame with a pair of gilded wings folded around it, glowing softly, centered in the picture, the same winged messengers as the Jacob’s Dream gallery. No head, no face, no arms, no body, no robe, no halo.

**The stone** (`stone.jpg`). Background: slate gray-blue, moonlit.
> A single smooth, rounded desert stone resting on sandy ground, warm gray with gold highlights on its top where a sleeper’s head once rested. A few tiny stones nearby. No face or features on the stone.

**Rashi** (`rashi.jpg`). Background: dark vineyard green.
> A medieval French Jewish scholar of about sixty, with a gray beard, a simple dark hood and robe, writing with a quill at a slanted desk. Grapevine leaves and a small cluster of grapes curl in from one side (tradition says Rashi kept vineyards in Troyes). Thoughtful, with a hint of a smile. No pointed hat.

**The Sages** (`sages.jpg`). Background: deep violet.
> Three rabbis of late antiquity in the Land of Israel, in tunics and draped mantles, seated close together around a low table with an open scroll. They are mid-argument and enjoying it: one points at the scroll, one raises a hand, one leans back smiling.

**Ramban** (`ramban.jpg`). Background: deep burgundy with faint gold Mudéjar geometric patterns.
> Nachmanides: a learned Catalan Jewish physician and rabbi of about sixty-five in the 13th century, with a full gray beard and a hooded scholar’s cloak, holding an open book against his chest. Calm, serious, kind eyes.

**Rambam** (`rambam.jpg`). Background: dark olive and sand.
> Maimonides: a Jewish philosopher and physician of about fifty in 12th-century Egypt, in a light turban and a long robe, with a dark beard. He holds an open book in one hand and a brass astrolabe in the other. Intelligent, patient, a teacher’s face.

## After a first try

Generate Jacob first. If he looks right, run the rest with the same settings so the set feels like one family of pictures. As with the gallery, retouch anything that drifts toward a halo, a face on the stone, or a human-looking angel.
