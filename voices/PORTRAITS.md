# Portraits for Voices of the Ladder

Ten portraits, one style, generated with the same image pipeline as the Jacob’s Dream gallery. The page shows each portrait in a round medallion with a double gold ring, so every prompt asks for a square picture with the face near the center, some space all around it, and no frame of its own. Anything that identifies a character, like Esau’s bow or Rebekah’s water jar, is held close to the face so the circle keeps it.

**Style: the gallery’s Illuminated Manuscript.** It suits everyone on the grid. Ramban and Rambam were both Sephardic, Rashi is medieval, and the style reads as clearly imagined, which matters because no one knows what Rashi, Ramban, or Rambam looked like. Jewish Papercut is the alternative if you want something folksier for the younger grades; I can rewrite the prompts for it.

**Where they go:** save each as a JPG, 1200 × 1200 pixels, named by id (`jacob.jpg`, `esau.jpg`, …) in `voices/portraits/`. I’ll connect them.

## The shared part of every prompt

Put this first, then the character’s own lines:

> Square 1:1 portrait. A miniature painting in the style of a 14th-century Sephardic Hebrew Bible from medieval Spain: flat jewel-toned pigments (lapis blue, vermilion, malachite green) with burnished gold leaf, fine black ink outlines, aged vellum texture. One figure, head and shoulders, with the face in the center of the picture and a comfortable margin on every side, because the picture will be cropped to a circle. Plain background: a flat field of the color named below with a few small gold stars. No frame, no border, no circle or medallion of its own. No halo and no gold disc behind anyone’s head. No crosses, no church architecture, no doves, no radiating rays. No words, letters, numbers, or writing of any kind; no signature.

## The ten characters

**Jacob** (`jacob.jpg`). Background: deep indigo night.
> A young traveler in a patterned wool cloak and a plain head cloth, with a short dark beard. He looks up and slightly away, tired but full of wonder, as if he has just woken from a dream. He holds a smooth gray stone against his chest.

**Esau** (`esau.jpg`). Background: warm red-brown, like earth at dusk.
> Jacob’s twin: a strong young outdoorsman with ruddy skin, thick reddish hair and beard, and a hunting bow slung over his shoulder. His expression is hurt and proud, not cruel: a man who has just lost something that mattered to him.

**Rebekah** (`rebekah.jpg`). Background: deep teal, like well water.
> A dignified woman in middle age, in a draped headscarf and patterned robe, with a clay water jar balanced on her shoulder. She looks into the distance with worried love, as if watching someone walk away.

**Isaac** (`isaac.jpg`). Background: warm plum-brown lamplight.
> An old man with a long white beard, his eyes softly closed because he can no longer see. His hands are raised to chest height, palms open, in the gesture of giving a blessing. Gentle, tired, kind.

**An angel** (`angel.jpg`). Background: deep blue night with more gold stars.
> Not a person at all: a single small golden flame with a pair of gilded wings folded around it, glowing softly in the center of the picture, the same winged messengers as the Jacob’s Dream gallery. No head, no face, no arms, no body, no robe, no halo.

**The stone** (`stone.jpg`). Background: slate gray-blue, moonlit.
> A single smooth, rounded desert stone in the center of the picture, resting on sandy ground, warm gray with gold highlights on its top where a sleeper’s head once rested. No face or features on the stone.

**Rashi** (`rashi.jpg`). Background: dark vineyard green.
> A medieval French Jewish scholar of about sixty, with a gray beard and a simple dark hood and robe. He holds a quill up near his face, as if he has just stopped writing to think. A few grapevine leaves curl in at one edge (tradition says Rashi kept vineyards in Troyes). Thoughtful, with a hint of a smile. No pointed hat.

**The Sages** (`sages.jpg`). Background: deep violet.
> Three rabbis of late antiquity in the Land of Israel, in tunics and draped mantles, seen head and shoulders and leaning close together over a scroll. They are mid-argument and enjoying it: one points, one raises a hand, one smiles.

**Ramban** (`ramban.jpg`). Background: deep burgundy with faint gold Mudéjar geometric patterns.
> Nachmanides: a learned Catalan Jewish physician and rabbi of about sixty-five in the 13th century, with a full gray beard and a hooded scholar’s cloak, holding an open book against his chest. Calm, serious, kind eyes.

**Rambam** (`rambam.jpg`). Background: dark olive and sand.
> Maimonides: a Jewish philosopher and physician of about fifty in 12th-century Egypt, in a light turban and a long robe, with a dark beard. He holds a brass astrolabe up beside his face. Intelligent, patient, a teacher’s face.

## After a first try

Generate Jacob first. If he looks right in the circle, run the rest with the same settings so the set feels like one family of pictures. As with the gallery, retouch anything that drifts toward a halo, a face on the stone, or a human-looking angel.
