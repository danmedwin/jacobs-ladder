# The stone’s visit: the script

A readable copy of what the tool says, for review. It is generated from `voices/data/stone.js`, so mark changes here and they will be carried into the data.

Small gray references are the source cards a teacher can open during the visit. Numbered lines are the steps the teacher moves through with Next.

## Grades K–2

*Short lines, pictures, and moving around. The teacher reads aloud.*

1. Hi! I’m the stone. I’m just one stone now. But I used to be lots of stones!
2. One night, Jacob came to our place. The sun had gone down, and he needed somewhere to sleep. <sub>Genesis 28:11</sub>
3. He was all alone, and he was afraid of wild animals. So he put us stones all around his head, to keep him safe. <sub>Rashi on Genesis 28:11 · B’reishit Rabbah 68:11</sub>
4. **Everybody moves: Be the stones!** Curl up small and round, like a stone. Now everybody, all together: “Pick me! Pick me!”
5. Then we started to argue! Every stone said, “Let this good man put his head on me!” <sub>Rashi on Genesis 28:11</sub>
6. **The stone asks:** Why did we all want to help Jacob?
   - Button **“He was tired”** → Yes! He walked a long way, and he needed a good rest.
   - Button **“He was all alone”** → Yes. When someone is all alone, it feels good to help.
   - Button **“He was a good person”** → That’s what we said! Every stone wanted to hold the head of a good person.
   - *Teacher: Let everyone answer out loud. Then choose what most of the group said.*
7. So God did something amazing. God made all of us into one big stone! <sub>Rashi on Genesis 28:11</sub>
8. **Everybody moves: Become one stone!** Everybody squeeze in close together… closer… closer! Now you’re all one big stone.
9. Jacob put his head on me and fell asleep. And right on top of me, he had his dream! <sub>Genesis 28:12</sub>
10. *[Animation: 3D Animated, from the Jacob’s Dream gallery.]*
11. In the morning, Jacob stood me up tall, like a pillar. And he poured oil on top of me, to show that this place was special. <sub>Genesis 28:18</sub>
12. **Everybody moves: Stand up tall!** Stand up straight and tall, like a stone pillar. Now hold very, very still!
13. Jacob gave our place a new name: Beit El. That means “House of God.” <sub>Genesis 28:19</sub>
14. **The stone asks the group to discuss:** What helps you feel safe when you go to sleep?
   - *Teacher: Take a few answers out loud. Then tap to hear the stone.*
   - Then the stone answers: For Jacob, it was me! And God, who told him, “I am with you.” <sub>Genesis 28:15</sub>
15. **The stone:** What would you like to ask me?
   1. *Were you comfy?* → Yes! The Sages said that under Jacob, we stones became as soft as a bed and a feather pillow. <sub>B’reishit Rabbah 68:11</sub>
   2. *Can stones really talk?* → Not really! This is a midrash, a story our teachers told about the Torah. It shows that everything wanted to help Jacob, even the stones. <sub>Rashi on Genesis 28:11</sub>
   3. *Who told your story?* → A teacher named Rashi wrote it down, about a thousand years ago. He learned it from even older books: the Talmud and the midrash. <sub>Rashi on Genesis 28:11 · Chullin 91b · B’reishit Rabbah 68:11</sub>
   4. *Where are you now?* → Nobody knows! But many years later, Jacob came back to Beit El and set up a stone there again. <sub>Genesis 35:14</sub>
16. **The rung:** “We all wanted to help Jacob, so God made us one.”
   - **The motion:** Make two fists and press them together into one.
17. Thanks for visiting me! Who do you want to meet next? *(Suggests: Jacob, An angel, Esau.)*
