Voices of the Ladder · Family learning morning

Printables

Everything to print for Sunday, November 22: the leaders’ guides, and what goes on every family table. Each one is a PDF, ready for the copier.

Scale

Print at 100%, “Actual size.” Not “Fit to page,” or the strips won’t fit the ladder.

Color

Color is best, for the faces and the gold. Black and white still works.

Paper

Letter, except the ladder sheet (11×17, also called tabloid or ledger). Cardstock for the table card, if you have it.

How many?

Change the numbers when the educator has a count. Each family gets its own table; the quantities below include a few spares.

    Cut the strips and the bedtime cards ahead of time; a paper cutter makes it quick. Fold the table cards along the middle so they stand like tents.
