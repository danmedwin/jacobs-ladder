Genesis 28:11–12

Jacob’s Dream

He came upon a certain place and stopped there for the night, for the sun had set. Taking one of the stones of that place, he put it under his head and lay down in that place. He had a dream; a stairway was set on the ground and its top reached to the sky, and angels of God were going up and down on it.

וַיִּפְגַּע בַּמָּקוֹם וַיָּלֶן שָׁם כִּי־בָא הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ וַיִּקַּח מֵאַבְנֵי הַמָּקוֹם וַיָּשֶׂם מְרַאֲשֹׁתָיו וַיִּשְׁכַּב בַּמָּקוֹם הַהוּא׃ וַיַּחֲלֹם וְהִנֵּה סֻלָּם מֻצָּב אַרְצָה וְרֹאשׁוֹ מַגִּיעַ הַשָּׁמָיְמָה וְהִנֵּה מַלְאֲכֵי אֱלֹהִים עֹלִים וְיֹרְדִים בּוֹ׃

Vayifga bamakom vayalen sham ki va hashemesh, vayikach mei'avnei hamakom vayasem m'ra'ashotav, vayishkav bamakom hahu. Vayachalom v'hineih sulam mutzav artzah v'rosho magia hashamaymah, v'hineih malachei Elohim olim v'yordim bo.

None of these images show God, per Jewish practice. Each stairway rises into sky and light. The Hebrew word for angels, malachim, also means messengers, so here they take the form of flames, birds, and light.
